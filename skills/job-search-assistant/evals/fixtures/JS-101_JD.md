Source: https://careers.northwind.example/jobs/101

Senior IT Program Manager, Cloud. Fully remote (United States).

Responsibilities
- Lead a portfolio of cloud migration programs from planning through cutover.
- Manage strategic vendors and their contracts.
- Own program budgets and report status to IT leadership.

Required qualifications
- 10+ years of experience delivering IT programs.
- Led migrations of applications to AWS.
- Vendor management, including contract negotiation.
- Managed program budgets of $5m or more.
- Agile delivery experience.

Preferred qualifications
- ITIL certification.
- ServiceNow experience.
- PMP certification.
