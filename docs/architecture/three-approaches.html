One job search, three ways to orchestrate it

The same problem (find leads, score fit against the full resume, show the gaps, tailor a resume) solved three ways. What changes between them is who decides the next step. What stays fixed is the data, the scoring rubric, the tracker rules and the evals, which is what lets you compare them fairly.

What the comparison shows a reader

  • Choosing who holds control. A puts it in the model, B in code, C splits it: the model decides what to ask, code computes the answer. The ADRs say why each fits its job.
  • Keeping the business rules in one place. The rubric and tracker rules are shared, so a new approach is a new front end, not a rewrite.
  • Measuring instead of asserting. The same synthetic persona and gold scores can run against each approach, and the eval results go in the repo.

Two pieces would make the comparison real rather than drawn: the shared rubric in core, and running the job-fit evals against both A and B. B's live-model path also needs its tool_choice fix before it can be measured. The milestones that get there are in the roadmap (docs/roadmap.md).
